# Chartwall – Play Store listing

**App name (30 max):** Chartwall: Trader Wallpaper

**Short description (80 max):**
Moving candlestick live wallpapers for traders, with your name and logo.

**Full description:**
Chartwall turns your phone into a trading desk. Pick a style and watch candles form, an order book breathe, or a heatmap shift right on your home and lock screen.

STYLES
• Candles – moving candlestick chart with EMA and volume
• Terminal – market watch list with live-looking tickers
• Order book – glowing bid and ask depth
• Heatmap – market tiles in green and red
• Fibonacci – price line with retracement levels
• Quote – your trading rule in big type

MAKE IT YOURS
• Crypto, forex or stocks symbols, or type your own (e.g. PSX:OGDC)
• Bullish, sideways or bearish mood
• Candle speed from still to fast
• Your own trading quote or rule
• Your name, company name and logo on the wallpaper
• Big-clock layout keeps the chart clear of large lock-screen clocks

BATTERY FRIENDLY
The animation pauses whenever the screen is off or an app covers the wallpaper.

CHARTWALL PRO (one-time purchase)
• All 6 styles and all 5 color themes (Neon, Gold, Ice, Violet, Mono)
• Company name and logo
• No watermark

Note: prices and charts are generated for design only. They are not live market data and not financial advice.
