Chartwall Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Chartwall is a live wallpaper app that draws trading-style charts on your phone. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Information we collect

Chartwall does not collect, store on our servers, sell or share any personal information. The app has no account, no analytics and no advertising.

Information that stays on your phone

The settings you choose (style, colors, symbol, quote, your name, your company name) and the logo image you pick are saved only inside the app's private storage on your device. They never leave your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Photos and files

When you upload a logo, Android's file picker lets you choose one image. The app copies only that image into its private storage. It cannot see your other photos or files. When you save a still wallpaper, the app writes one image to your Pictures/Chartwall folder.

Purchases

Chartwall Pro is sold through Google Play. Payments are handled entirely by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. The app only receives a confirmation that Pro was bought; it never sees your card or payment details.

Market data

Prices and charts in Chartwall are generated for design only. They are not live market data and are not financial advice.

Children

Chartwall is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/mmuhammad3077-dev/chartwall/issues.
